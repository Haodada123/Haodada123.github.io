About Hao Nie

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Peng Xu.

My research lies broadly in security and privacy, with a focus on the security and efficiency of encrypted and private data retrieval. I am particularly interested in encrypted search, leakage analysis, private information retrieval (PIR), and privacy-preserving search.

Research

I am currently working on the following research directions:

  • Leakage Analysis and Attacks. Understanding how observable leakage from encrypted databases can be exploited to recover sensitive queries and data.
  • Leakage-Resilient Encrypted Search. Designing practical encrypted databases that mitigate exploitable leakage while retaining efficient search and dynamic updates.
  • Private Data Retrieval. Developing efficient private information retrieval (PIR) and privacy-preserving retrieval protocols for large-scale databases.
  • Secure Payments for LPSI in Data Trading. Investigating secure payment mechanisms as part of an ongoing industry-collaborative innovation project.
