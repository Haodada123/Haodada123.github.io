About

Security for practical encrypted retrieval

I am a Ph.D. student in Cybersecurity at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research focuses on understanding how information leakage affects encrypted retrieval systems in realistic settings and on designing efficient mechanisms that reduce or eliminate such leakage.

My recent work studies leakage-abuse attacks against searchable symmetric encryption (SSE) and dynamic SSE (DSSE), including query-recovery attacks under intermittent observations. Beyond attacks, I am interested in practical, low-overhead protections for encrypted search and privacy-preserving retrieval.

Research

Research interests

Bridging rigorous security analysis with systems that remain efficient enough to deploy.

01

Searchable Encryption

Security analysis and design of searchable symmetric encryption for practical encrypted databases.

02

Leakage-Abuse Attacks

Query recovery, leakage correlation, and realistic attacker models for SSE and dynamic SSE.

03

Private Information Retrieval

Privacy-preserving retrieval mechanisms that balance security, communication, and computation costs.

04

Secure Vector Retrieval

Exploring secure retrieval techniques for AI-driven vector search and similarity-based applications.
