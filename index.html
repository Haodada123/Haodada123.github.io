About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Cybersecurity at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research focuses on understanding how information leakage affects encrypted retrieval systems in realistic settings and on designing efficient mechanisms that reduce or eliminate such leakage.

My recent work studies leakage-abuse attacks against searchable symmetric encryption (SSE) and dynamic SSE (DSSE), including query-recovery attacks under intermittent observations. Beyond attacks, I am interested in practical, low-overhead protections for encrypted search and privacy-preserving retrieval.

🔐

Research interests

Searchable Encryption
