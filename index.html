Profile

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Cybersecurity at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research investigates how information leakage affects encrypted retrieval systems in realistic settings, and how secure retrieval mechanisms can be designed to remain both practical and privacy-preserving.

I am especially interested in searchable symmetric encryption (SSE), dynamic SSE, private information retrieval, and secure retrieval techniques for emerging AI-oriented workloads such as vector search.

Research Focus

Leakage Analysis Query Recovery Dynamic SSE Privacy-Preserving Retrieval
  • Understand the real-world security risks caused by access-pattern and query leakage.
  • Develop leakage-abuse attacks against SSE and DSSE under realistic observation models.
  • Design defenses that improve confidentiality without sacrificing efficiency.
  • Explore future secure retrieval systems for encrypted and AI-driven search scenarios.
