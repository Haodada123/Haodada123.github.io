About Hao Nie

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Cyber Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My research lies at the intersection of systems security and applied cryptography, with a focus on searchable encryption, private information retrieval, and leakage-abuse attacks.

I study the privacy risks created by access-pattern and query leakage in encrypted retrieval systems. My goal is to understand these risks under realistic observation models and to build retrieval mechanisms that remain both secure and practical.

Research

I am currently interested in the following topics:

  • Encrypted search: practical and privacy-preserving searchable symmetric encryption.
  • Leakage analysis: query recovery and leakage-abuse attacks against SSE and DSSE.
  • Private retrieval: secure retrieval for databases, vector search, and emerging AI workloads.
